# System Glossary & Canonical Register

Canonical definitions, physical parameters, assumption registers, and verification gate definitions for the SIH 26 Mine Subsidence Early-Warning System (Part 1 Finale Build).

---

## 1. Domain & Physical Concepts

### Knothe Influence Theory
- **Knothe Model:** Empirical-mathematical formulation modelling surface subsidence as a Gaussian influence function integrated over the mined rectangular panel footprint.
- **Subsidence Factor ($a$):** Ratio of maximum theoretical subsidence to extracted seam thickness ($S_{max\_full} / m$). Dimensionless ($0 < a \le 1$). Repinned in [[work-packages/WP0-data-pinning|WP0]].
- **Angle of Draw ($\beta$) / Influence Angle:** Angle between the vertical at the panel extraction boundary and the limit of measurable surface movement. Characterized by $\tan \beta$.
- **Radius of Principal Influence ($r$):** Distance from the extraction edge to the limit of surface deformation:
  $$r = \frac{H}{\tan \beta}$$
  where $H$ is the extraction depth. For Adriyala baseline ($H = 375\text{ m}, \tan \beta = 2.0$), $r = 187.5\text{ m}$.
- **Subcritical Panel:** A panel whose width $W < 2r$. Because $250\text{ m} < 375\text{ m}$, Adriyala LW1 is subcritical, meaning surface subsidence never reaches $a \cdot m$. Peak subsidence under baseline defaults is $1630\text{ mm}$ versus $1800\text{ mm}$ theoretical.
- **Time Coefficient ($c$):** Knothe time factor parameter defining settling rate over time ($1 - e^{-c \cdot t}$). Measured in $\text{day}^{-1}$. $t_{63} = 1/c$ represents the time to achieve $63.2\%$ of final subsidence.
- **Travelling Profile Cross:** The optimal survey and node monitoring layout consisting of one transverse line across the panel deformation extent plus one longitudinal line along the active extraction window, intersecting at a shared crossing node.

### Kinematic Derivatives
- **Subsidence ($S$):** Vertical downward ground displacement, measured in **millimetres (mm)**, positive downward in physics outputs.
- **Tilt ($T$):** First spatial derivative of subsidence ($\partial S / \partial x, \partial S / \partial y$), measured in **microradians ($\mu\text{rad}$)**. Maximizes near the inflection point ($y = \pm W/2$).
- **Curvature ($K$):** Second spatial derivative ($\partial^2 S / \partial x^2, \partial^2 S / \partial y^2$), measured in **$1/\text{km}$**. Zero at the inflection point, concave upward outside (tension), concave downward inside (compression).
- **Horizontal Strain ($\epsilon$):** Ground deformation over a physical baseline:
  $$\epsilon = \frac{\Delta L}{L}$$
  Measured in **microstrain ($\mu\epsilon$)**. Computed strictly as a **finite difference** over baseline $L$ ($10\text{ m}$ or $30\text{ m}$), NOT a mathematical point derivative, matching the physical physics of rod and wire extensometers.
- **Horizontal Displacement ($U$):** Lateral ground movement toward the centre of the subsidence basin, measured in **millimetres (mm)**.

## 2. Provenance Taxonomy

Every reading and emitted value in [[docs/interface-contracts|contracts]] and `nodes.csv` must carry exactly one of three immutable provenance tags (Invariant 6):

| Tag | Canonical Definition | Where Applied |
|---|---|---|
| `real` | Value is directly sourced from a digitized field survey monument reading at a verified physical location and epoch. | Sourced from Adriyala JMMF paper (`10.18311/jmmf/2022/32099`) at exact monument coordinates and dates. |
| `pinned` | Value generated by model equations whose governing parameters were fitted against real field data, evaluated at a location/epoch where real data exists. | Knothe model outputs at monument locations between survey dates. |
| `synthetic` | Value generated from spatial/temporal interpolation, mathematical derivation (tilt, curvature, strain), sensor noise models, battery models, or packet loss simulations. | **All tilt, strain, and displacement values** (no field time series exists), and all off-grid interpolated values. |

> [!CAUTION]
> There is **no fourth tag** and no `unknown`. Emitting an untagged or invalid value raises `ProvenanceError` (Gate [[gates/G04-provenance-tags-on-every-row|G04]]).

## 4. Radio & Mesh Network Parameters

- **IN865 Band:** Indian sub-GHz license-free frequency allocation (865–867 MHz) governed by GSR 564(E).
- **Spreading Factor (SF):** Orthogonal chirp modulation rate. Scouts transmit to Anchors at **SF7** ($61.7\text{ ms}$ airtime for 23 B). Anchors transmit backbone bundles to Gateway at **SF8** ($297.5\text{ ms}$ airtime for 98 B).
- **Airtimes (Authoritative Table):**
  - Scout 23 B uplink: **$61.7\text{ ms}$** (SF7) / $113.2\text{ ms}$ (SF8).
  - Anchor 98 B bundle (6 children): **$297.5\text{ ms}$** (SF8).
  - Anchor 6 B Bitmap ACK: **$36.1\text{ ms}$** (SF7).
  - Gateway Beacon: **$82.4\text{ ms}$** (SF8).
- **Duty Cycle Ceiling:** **$\le 1.0\%$** self-imposed engineering limit across all tiers. GSR 564(E) mandates 1 W ERP and 200 kHz bandwidth, NOT duty cycle; the 1% cap is an LPWAN engineering best practice (Gate [[gates/G08-anchor-duty-cycle-under-one-percent|G08]]).
- **Superframe:** 60.0-second repeating TDMA cycle:
  - $t = 0.0\text{ s}$: Gateway Beacon & time sync broadcast.
  - $t = 2.0\text{ s} - 11.5\text{ s}$: Scout uplink window (4 local channels, $92\text{ ms}$ slot = $61.7\text{ ms}$ airtime + $30.3\text{ ms}$ guard).
  - $t = 12.0\text{ s}$: Anchor broadcast single 6-byte bitmap ACK.
  - $t = 13.0\text{ s}$: Anchor $\to$ Gateway bundle on 2 backbone channels.
  - $t = 15.5\text{ s}$: Emergency window (8 sub-slots of $92\text{ ms} = 736\text{ ms}$).
  - $t = 16.5\text{ s} - 60.0\text{ s}$: Deep sleep across all tiers.
- **Dedup Key:** **`(node_id, epoch)`** strictly. Sequence number `seq` resets on node reboot and is diagnostic only (Gate [[gates/G07-dedup-by-node-and-epoch|G07]]).
- **Emergency Sub-Slot:** Driven by **`node.child_index`** ($0..7$). Never `node_id % 16`, which causes catastrophic hash collisions among orphaned sibling nodes (Gates [[gates/G10-emergency-slot-from-child-index|G10]], [[gates/G11-no-emergency-subslot-collisions|G11]]).

## 5. World-State Engine Mechanics

- **Integer-Millimetre Grid (`int32`):** Ground deformation accumulation is calculated and logged strictly as `int32` millimetres ($dz_{mm} \in \mathbb{Z}$). Floating point addition drifts over $16,500$ hourly timesteps in a 690-day simulation. Integers never drift (Gate [[gates/G06-bit-identical-terrain-replay|G06]]).
- **Delta Log (`terrain_changes.jsonl`):** Monotonically ordered log of non-zero cell movements per epoch. Zero-movement epochs log an empty list `cells: []` to guarantee dense, gapless epoch progression.
- **Replay Guarantee:** Starting from `terrain_state.npz` at $t=0$ and applying deltas sequentially yields an exact, bit-identical match (`np.array_equal`) at time $T$.
