### F10 — cracks, damage, vibration

**The headline finding is what did *not* need fixing.** Adarsh asked to fix the maths and copy the old `sih26/simulation` sandbox. Checked first, and it runs the other way: the sandbox is an *unfitted* model of a *different assumed* mine (tan β 1.9 assumed vs 2.5676 fitted; a 0.75 vs 0.45 fitted; c 0.04 vs 0.02358 fitted; no inflection offset vs 58.96 m fitted; fixed mask vs travelling face). Copying its constants would move every validated number off the field data without failing a test.

The sandbox's warning that finite-differencing S injects a fake strain signal was also checked and **does not bite here**: curvature is identical to 5 significant figures across step sizes 0.02 → 2.0 m, both transversely and across the face, and the largest second difference between adjacent transect points is 0.000279 1/km — no spike at the clip boundaries. Cost is not an issue either (strain over the full grid: 12.3 ms/day). So `tilt`, `curvature`, `strain` were left alone.

**The one real gap, closed:** `curvature()` returned only the diagonal, so there was no `∂²S/∂x∂y`, no principal strain and therefore **no crack direction**. Added `curvature_xy`, `principal_strain` (Mohr's circle on B × the curvature tensor), and `horizontal_displacement_factor` so B has one definition shared with `displacement()`. Measured at day 690: trough floor e2 = −13225 µε (compression), rib e1 = +6473 µε at θ = 90° (cracks parallel to the rib), panel corner θ = **40.65°** — off-axis, which is the whole reason the cross term was needed. Trace invariant holds to 3.4e-13.

**B is the only unfitted constant and cracks are linear in it.** It cannot be fitted from the JMMF data (vertical subsidence only). So `width_sensitivity()` reports the band: max opening 22.05 mm at 0.35 r → 28.63 mm at 0.40 r, a 30 % spread.

New `cracks.py` (latched opening over an explicit crack spacing, partial closure, NCB change-of-length damage) and `vibration.py` (blast scaled-distance law, caving driven by face advance, DGMS frequency-banded limits, one narrow crack-trigger coupling).
