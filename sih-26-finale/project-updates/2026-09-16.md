### Question 6 — why the subsidence travels instead of sitting still
**It is correct, and it is the whole point of a longwall.** LW1 is a 250 m × 2500 m retreat panel advancing
**4.0 m/day** (`config/mines/adriyala_lw1.yaml`). The face is a moving 250 m line, not a fixed point, so the
trough is a wave that follows it for 625 days end to end. Verified on the exported frames: day 345 → face at
**1,380 m** = 4.0 × 345 exactly; play on to day 499 → face at **1,997 m**. Subsidence lags the face and never
leads it (F6 §2: reaches 18 m ahead of the face, exactly 0 at face + 100 m). A single fixed bowl would mean a
bord-and-pillar collapse, which is a different mine and a different model.
