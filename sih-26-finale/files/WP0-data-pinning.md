## Goal

Replace guessed Knothe parameters with parameters **fitted to real measured ground movement**, and record the fit residual so the error is a known number rather than an unknown one.

## Why this exists

Without it the project has a closed validation loop: synthetic data generated by a formula, then evaluated against the same formula. A model trained on that output learns the simulator, not the ground. Provenance tagging becomes decoration — everything is `synthetic` and nothing is `real`.

There is also a concrete, already-detected error. Adriyala LW1 measured a maximum subsidence of **1.267 m, or 19.5% of seam height**. The baseline pair `m = 3.0 m, a = 0.6` predicts 1.63 m and implies `S_max/m = 0.54`. Both cannot be true. The system was about to generate 690 days of training data off a subsidence factor that is wrong by roughly a factor of three.

## Primary source

**Ramalingeswarudu, Dundra, Sastry & Murty (2022)**, "Analysis of Surface Subsidence and Relative Movements of Underground Rock Strata over an Inclined Longwall Panel", *Journal of Mines, Metals and Fuels* 70(9):484–491. DOI `10.18311/jmmf/2022/32099`. Open access, CC BY-NC — legal to use and cite.

What it contains:

| | |
|---|---|
| Site | Adriyala Project Area, Ramagundam Region, SCCL — longwall panel no. 1 |
| Panel | 250 m face length, 2333 m gate roadway |
| Seam | Inclined 10° (1 in 5.5) toward the tailgate |
| Max subsidence | 1.267 m, 19.5% of seam height |
| Monitoring | 80 paths across the major transverse section, 13 paths along the panel length |
| Monuments | one every 30 m along each path |
| Sampling | easting, northing and elevation recorded at 30-day intervals |
| Reported | one major transverse path analysed out to 690 days |
| Extra | MPBX at panel centre, roof bed separation correlated with surface subsidence |
| Figures needed | Fig. 6 (subsidence profile at different stages), Fig. 7 (profile along path S) |
| Tables needed | Table 1 (panel details), Table 2 (mining parameters), Table 3 (profile function parameters) |

**Note the monitoring layout.** A transverse line plus a longitudinal line of fixed-spacing points is exactly the §3.2 travelling profile cross, which was derived independently from the Knothe sampling-error curve. That convergence is a pitch slide, not a coincidence to bury.

## Secondary source — mine-independence

A second panel from a different coalfield, so the simulator is proven not to be Adriyala-shaped. Preferred: a US Bureau of Mines / NIOSH longwall subsidence report from `stacks.cdc.gov` — public domain, no licence question, and monument readings are tabulated rather than only plotted. The southern Illinois longwall study covers vertical and horizontal deformations of sixty-five monuments along and across the study panel, with underground convergence and roof-sag instrumentation alongside.

Alternative if that proves awkward: *JMMF* 70(2), Feb 2022, "A study of subsidence over a longwall panel – prediction and calibration" (Chitti Ravi Kiran, Mishra, Venkataramayya) — same open-access journal, different Indian panel.

**Two datasets. Stop there.** A third is scope creep.

## The honest constraint

No downloadable CSV of longwall subsidence exists. The data lives inside figures in papers. Digitisation is unavoidable — roughly two hours with WebPlotDigitizer, not a research project.

And: **no real strain or tilt time series exists** for the Adriyala surface. Those are derivatives of the fitted surface and are tagged `synthetic`, permanently. State this rather than letting anyone assume otherwise.

## Work

### Step 1 — Read Table 1 (D1)

Extract seam extraction height and confirm working depth. Fill `A3_seam_thickness_m` in `config/mines/adriyala_lw1.yaml`.

Also resolve the **depth conflict**: 375 m appears in the strata-control literature for the Adriyala Longwall Project, 410 m appears in a subsidence study of the same panel. Take the JMMF paper's figure and note which one the others disagree with.

### Step 2 — Digitise (D2)

WebPlotDigitizer on Fig. 6 and Fig. 7. One curve per reported epoch.

Output `data/real/adriyala_lw1_profiles.csv`:

```
path_id,path_type,distance_m,epoch_days,subsidence_mm,source,digitised_by
S,transverse,-300,30,-2,10.18311/jmmf/2022/32099,adarsh
S,transverse,-270,30,-5,10.18311/jmmf/2022/32099,adarsh
```

Conventions: `distance_m` is signed, zero at the panel centre. `subsidence_mm` is negative downward. Sample at 30 m to match the real monument spacing — do not interpolate to a finer grid, that manufactures precision the source does not have.

Second dataset into `data/real/illinois_panel_profiles.csv`, same schema.

### Step 3 — Fit (D3)

`src/minesim/fitting.py`. Least-squares fit of `physics.subsidence` against the digitised CSV, solving for `a`, `tan β`, `c`.

Output `data/fitted/adriyala_lw1_params.json`:

```json
{
  "mine": "adriyala_lw1",
  "source_doi": "10.18311/jmmf/2022/32099",
  "fitted_at": "2026-09-16",
  "params": {"subsidence_factor": 0.0, "tan_beta": 0.0, "time_coefficient_per_day": 0.0},
  "fit": {
    "rms_residual_mm": 0.0,
    "max_residual_mm": 0.0,
    "n_points": 0,
    "n_epochs": 0,
    "r_squared": 0.0
  },
  "residual_by_flank": {"dip_side_rms_mm": 0.0, "rise_side_rms_mm": 0.0},
  "notes": ""
}
```

Write the fitted values into `config/mines/adriyala_lw1.yaml`, replacing the `null`s. That is the moment `load_config()` stops raising.

### Step 4 — Judge the fit (D4)

`rms_residual_mm` against the A10 detection threshold of 10 mm and the 50 m sampling error of 32.6 mm:

| Residual | Verdict | Action |
|---|---|---|
| < 50 mm | Knothe is a good driver | Proceed as planned |
| 50–150 mm | Usable but weak | Proceed, report the number, flag for Part 2's loss weighting |
| > 150 mm | Knothe is the wrong driver | **Escalate.** The paper derives its own profile function (Table 3) — use that as the terrain driver and keep Knothe as the detector only |

That last row is the reason this step exists before WP3 rather than after. Finding out on D8 that the terrain driver is wrong costs the build.

Report `residual_by_flank` separately. The seam is inclined 10° with a **higher angle of draw on the dip side**, and the WP1 model is symmetric, so a systematic one-sided residual is expected and is not a bug. Do not tune it away — list asymmetric draw as a v2 item.

### Step 5 — Second mine (D8)

Repeat steps 2–4 for the Illinois panel. Fill `config/mines/illinois_lw.yaml`. Run the full pipeline on it unchanged. If any code change is needed to make a second mine work, G15 has failed and the sizing algorithm is not mine-independent.

## Definition of done — G0

- [ ] No `null` remains in `config/mines/adriyala_lw1.yaml`
- [ ] `data/real/adriyala_lw1_profiles.csv` exists with ≥ 3 epochs and ≥ 20 points per epoch
- [ ] `data/fitted/adriyala_lw1_params.json` exists with a real `rms_residual_mm`
- [ ] Every value in `params` traces to a fit against measured data, not a guess
- [ ] `load_config()` returns a valid `Config` without raising
- [ ] The fit verdict is recorded and, if > 150 mm, escalated before WP3 proceeds
- [ ] Second mine repeated, pipeline runs unchanged

## Do not

- Fill a `null` with a plausible-looking number to unblock a lane. The crash is the feature.
- Interpolate digitised points to a finer grid than 30 m.
- Tag anything derived from the fit as `real`. Fitted model output at a monument position is `pinned`; everything else is `synthetic`.
- Tune the model to remove the dip-side residual.
- Add a third dataset.
