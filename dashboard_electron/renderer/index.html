DGMS TECHNICAL REFERENCE MANUAL • ADRIYALA LONGWALL PANEL 1 • KNOTHE–LITWINISZYN GROUND MODEL
R4 continuously monitors longwall extraction panels via a surface mesh of 31 wireless sensor pegs and an autonomous, deterministic gateway detector. This manual details physical transducer assignments, ground-truth threshold tiers, second-derivative basin mechanics, and the 7-stage veto gate pipeline.

[01] • SENSOR NODES & NETWORK TOPOLOGY ESP32 • 868MHz LORA

Every sensor node utilizes standardized hardware — an ESP32 microcontroller, SX1276 LoRa transceiver, and seven transducers enclosed in a weatherproof housing mounted on a survey peg. On the dashboard map, symbol shape designates architectural role and color reflects live state.

NODE ROLES & SPATIAL ASSIGNMENTS
ROLE COUNT SPATIAL LOCATION FUNCTIONAL RESPONSIBILITY
SCOUT 25 Inner ring, across longwall panel Primary sensing pegs measuring strain, extensometer delta, bi-axial tilt, and vibration. Drawn on map as a circle.
ANCHOR 5 Middle ring, outside influence zone Geotechnical datum pegs constructed outside draw angle. Must read zero displacement. Movement indicates sensor drift, used for common-mode noise cancellation.
GATEWAY 1 Outer perimeter hut, 10m mast Collects LoRa packets, runs real-time Knothe detector, triggers sirens & SMS dispatches, buffers telemetry to local SD if backhaul drops. Fixed coordinate sink.
LIVE NODE STATES & ACTION PROTOCOLS
STATE HMI BADGE TRIGGER CRITERIA OPERATOR PROTOCOL
ACTIVE ACTIVE Reporting on schedule, readings within baseline variance. Normal operations. No intervention required.
WARNING WARNING Strain or tilt elevated (|z-score| > 3) without network quorum. Monitor trend closely. Awaiting spatial corroboration by adjacent scouts.
CRITICAL CRITICAL Node exceeded Level 2 tensile/tilt threshold with cluster quorum. Verify alarm panel, inspect subsidence zone, notify shift supervisor.
LAST-GASP LAST-GASP Node emitted high-priority power-loss or mount-fracture packet. Treat as strong localized physical evidence. Dispatch surface patrol.
DEAD DEAD No telemetry packet received for 3+ cycles (> 180s). Check RF link. If multiple adjacent nodes drop simultaneously, cluster collapse rule applies.
HARDWARE TIERS & SAMPLING DENSITY
The Tier descriptor (1A, 1B, 1C, 2A, 2B, 3) denotes sampling density and physical hardware package. Tier 1 nodes sit on steep trough inflection margins at dense 25m spacing where surface curvature κ changes fastest; the flat interior panel is sampled coarsely at ~75m. Spacing is mathematically derived from the Knothe influence radius (r ≈ 75–197m depending on extraction depth), ensuring no subsidence trough can slip between pegs undetected.
[02] • THE SEVEN SENSORS ON EACH NODE TELEMETRY MATRIX

Each sensor node integrates seven distinct physical channels. To eliminate false alarms, each transducer has a specific role in detection — primary detector, voting corroborator, spectral discriminator, or thermal compensation.

SENSOR TYPE TELEMETRY KEY DETECTOR ROLE PHYSICAL OPERATING PRINCIPLE
Foil Strain Gauge strain_ue Primary Trigger Measures surface tensile/compressive strain (με) across ground anchors. Yields highest signal-to-noise ratio (~2200:1).
Wire Extensometer ext_delta_mm Primary Trigger Measures 3D baseline displacement between adjacent pegs 10m apart, directly quantifying tensile surface tear.
Bi-axial Inclinometer tilt_x, tilt_y Secondary Vote Monitors ground slope in millidegrees (mdeg). Thermal drift can rival signals at distance, so tilt cannot trigger an alarm without strain quorum.
Conductive Crackmeter fissure_mm / bits Physical Fissure Latch Conductive break-trace that snaps upon fissure opening. Retains latched state even if node power subsequently fails.
Vibration Analyzer vib_rms, vib_fdom Acoustic Discriminator Dominant frequency vetoes artificial noise: 40–80 Hz (blasting), 8–20 Hz (haul trucks), 50 Hz (conveyors), while preserving 100–250 Hz true rock-fracture energy.
Die Thermometer die_temp_c Thermal Compensation Calibrates silicon temperature drift out of MEMS inclinometer readings across diurnal temperature cycles.
Battery Monitor vbat_mv Power Health Diagnostic Corrects voltage-sag measurement errors and flags nodes nearing brownout before last-gasp packet emission.
[03] • GROUND ZONES & MAP OVERLAYS 6×6 GRID • 36 ZONES

The mine surface is divided into a 6×6 grid of 36 zones (each 100×100m). A zone is a spatial ground tile whose geotechnical deformation state is evaluated independently. Zones drive the SURFACE shading overlays on the 2D and 3D maps and constitute the fundamental spatial unit for alarm generation.

ZONE GROUND-TRUTH LADDER
ZONE STATE ALARM PHYSICS THRESHOLD GEOTECHNICAL MEANING
STABLE — Displacement S < 0.05m No measurable subsidence. Baseline state. Never alarms.
SETTLING — S ≥ 0.05m, strain low Uniform downward subsidence without dangerous tensile stretch. Normal longwall trough behavior.
TENSION LEVEL 1 ε ≥ 4.0 mm/m or κ ≥ 0.9×10⁻⁴/m Surface ground stretched near tensile capacity. Tension cracking imminent along trough margins.
CRITICAL LEVEL 2 ε ≥ 5.3 mm/m or κ ≥ 1.5×10⁻⁴/m Tensile limit breached. Surface fissure formation confirmed. Operator dispatch triggered.
FAILED LEVEL 3 Step drop ΔS ≥ 0.30m or κ ≥ 4.0×10⁻⁴/m Sudden void breach, sinkhole, or goaf collapse. Immediate evacuation condition.
Irreversible Hysteresis Latch: Ground deformation is physically irreversible on operative timescales. Once a zone ascends to CRITICAL, it remains latched at CRITICAL until a manual supervisor reset or simulation rewind. This latch prevents noisy threshold oscillations from flooding the alarm journal.

[04] • SUBSIDENCE BASIN MECHANICS & PHYSICS KNOTHE-LITWINISZYN FORMULATION

Operators frequently query why a localized roof fall lights up so much of the map. This behavior reflects real-world geomechanical physics rather than software over-sensitivity:

KNOTHE INFLUENCE RADIUS: r = H / tan(β) ≈ 75m – 197m (where H = panel depth, β = draw angle)
TOTAL BASIN SPAN: W_basin = W_panel + 2 × r ≈ 250m + 2(197m) = 644m > 600m map viewport
TENSILE STRAIN TENSOR: ε(x) = -z • (d²S / dx²) [Peak tension occurs on outer rim, not trough center]
  1. Subsidence Basin Exceeds the Field Window: The panel is 250m wide and the Knothe influence zone extends ~197m past each rib line, so the regional subsidence trough spans ~644m. The monitoring window is 600×600m; thus regional deformation naturally touches all 36 zones.
  2. Strain and Curvature Peak on the Perimeter Rim: Strain is the second derivative of vertical subsidence (κ = d²S/dx²). While maximum vertical drop occurs at the center of extraction, maximum tensile stretch occurs on the outer rim / inflection boundary. Consequently, peripheral zones breach tension thresholds while central zones experience compression.
  3. Superposition on Moving Strata: Sudden roof falls superimpose their local subsidence delta onto the already active regional trough, pushing settled zones across the critical threshold simultaneously.
[05] • ALARM DISPATCH MATRIX & ESCALATION DGMS PROTOCOL

Alarms are generated deterministically per zone per state entered (e.g. ALM-Z13-TENSION, ALM-Z13-CRITICAL, ALM-Z13-FAILED). An alarm is keyed on zone + state and can never repeat for the same transition. A cluster of alarms represents the propagation front of a single geomechanical incident.

LEVEL SOURCE ZONE CONDITION AUTOMATED DISPATCH MANDATORY OPERATOR ACTION
LEVEL 1 TENSION (ε ≥ 4.0 mm/m) Dashboard visual highlight, status bar alert, shift log entry. Acknowledge alarm. Verify anchor stability and inspect adjacent scout trends.
LEVEL 2 CRITICAL (ε ≥ 5.3 mm/m) SMS dispatch to safety contacts, audio warning, map auto-focus. Verify spatial extent, alert underground mining engineer, dispatch surface patrol.
LEVEL 3 FAILED (ΔS ≥ 0.30m) Emergency horn/siren activated, urgent SMS, red lockout banner. Initiate emergency withdrawal protocol for longwall face. Evacuate affected surface sector.
[06] • 7-STAGE GATEWAY DETECTOR PIPELINE DETERMINISTIC VETO GATES

The gateway runs a deterministic 7-stage vetting pipeline on every calculation cycle. No machine-learning or black-box models exist in the critical alarm path:

01 • Staleness Gate GATE 1
Rejects stale data. If the newest telemetry is older than 3 cycles (> 180s), the gateway emits STALE rather than ALL-CLEAR.
CRITERIA: Data Age ≤ 3 cycles
02 • Quorum Gate GATE 2
Prevents rogue sensors from alarming. Requires at least 5 nodes with strain z-scores exceeding 3.0 before an incident can trigger.
CRITERIA: ≥ 5 nodes with |z| > 3.0
03 • Knothe Bowl Fit GATE 3
Fits a least-squares Knothe influence bowl to the active displacement vector. Scatter with poor geometric correlation is vetoed.
CRITERIA: Model R² ≥ 0.85
04 • DGMS Blast Veto GATE 4
Cross-checks statutory blast logs. Displacements within 120s and 500m of a registered shot that rapidly decay are vetoed.
CRITERIA: Time ≤ 120s, Dist ≤ 500m
05 • Machine Veto GATE 5
Spectral filtering notches out 50Hz electrical noise and 8–20Hz heavy haul truck harmonics while admitting 100–250Hz rock crack energy.
CRITERIA: Notched 50Hz, Veto 8-20Hz
06 • Cluster Silence GATE 6
When ≥ 3 adjacent nodes drop off the air together, it is classified as physical ground shear unless lightning is logged within 5km.
CRITERIA: ≥ 3 dead scouts in cluster
07 • Persistence Gate GATE 7
Anomaly readings must persist across ≥ 3 consecutive sampling cycles unless backed by a latched crackmeter or last-gasp packet.
CRITERIA: 3 cycles OR crack latched
[07] • 90-DAY REPLAY CONTROLLER & FORENSICS POST-INCIDENT RECONSTRUCTION

The Replay Controller plays back archived PostgreSQL telemetry through the identical visualization engine used for live telemetry:

  • FROM / TO Time Windows: Sets the playback interval. (Note: simulation clocks frequently run ahead of real-time; clear date fields to playback contemporary events).
  • Speed Multipliers: 10X, 50X, and 100X allow reviewing an 8-hour shift in under five minutes.
  • Node Kill & Alarm Timeline: Correlates sensor dropouts against alarm progression to establish the ground rupture sequence.
  • False-Positive Sweep: Systematically reviews past anomalies against blast and machinery logs to audit detector efficacy.
