**1. The engine is not fitted to the team's own real data.**
- `sandbox/constants.py:37-40` uses m 3.0 (ASSUMED), a 0.75, tanβ 1.9, c 0.04/d, and the face moves 5 m/d (`:102`).
- The fit to 283 digitised Adriyala points (JMMF 2022, DOI 10.18311/jmmf/2022/32099, 6 survey epochs from day 210 to 690) is in `sih-26-finale/mine-sim/data/fitted/adriyala_lw1_params.json`:
  - a 0.45, tanβ 2.5676, c 0.02358/d, m 3.6, **inflection offset 58.96 m**, face 4 m/d
  - RMS 52 mm, R² 0.985
- Result: the engine peaks at **1997 mm**, but the mine measured **1267 mm**.

**2. With today's engine, a 365-day year is flat for its last half.**
- The face leaves the 600 m window on day 159.5.
- S is at 99% of its final value by day 180, and no node moves ≥ 1 mm/d after day 268.

**3. One real panel leaves half the nodes quiet.**
- I ran the real fit on the 31-node layout (`sandbox/layout.py:89`) in-memory.
- **One panel:** peak 1202 mm, which matches the fit's 1201.7 mm, so the calculation is right. The ground moves from day 42 to 322, but only 16 of 30 sensing nodes see ≥ 50 mm.
- **3 panels** (across-strike centres −290, 0, +290 m, which is 250 m + a 40 m chain pillar; face starts on days −120, 0, +120):
  - all 30 sensing nodes see ≥ 50 mm and tilt ≥ 3 mm/m
  - max strain 10.4 mm/m, and the ground moves from day 0 to 362
  - So the ground itself makes every node alarm; nothing needs to be faked.
- `minesim/physics.py:56` (finale) already implements this district model in closed form, with `y_offsets_m` and `start_day_offsets_d`. It imports and runs under `simulation/.venv`, so we can reuse it and cross-check against it.

**4. What makes the obvious approach wrong: nothing reads the data to raise alarms today.**
- `session.py` (comment above `_assign_node_states`, around `:430`) says only operator events redden a node, and "Knothe subsidence … leaves the board green".
- A data-driven year on today's code would therefore stay green all year.
- The engine also **writes fake values**:
  - `session.py:454-461` forces `strain_ue` ≥ 6500 (CRITICAL) and ≥ 4200 (WARNING), even on tiers with no strain gauge
  - `mqtt_bridge.py:331-337,425-430` hard-codes `trough_fit_r2` 0.94/0.88, confidence 0.92 and `days_to_level_3` 3.5
- A dataset recorded from today's session output would carry these fake values into the dataset.

**5. The alarms are not professional.** No C7/C8 engine exists in code.
- **Two alarms for one condition.** Zone and node alarms are published for the same nodes, with conflicting levels: a zone at CRITICAL is level 2, a node at CRITICAL is level 3.
- **Every alarm arrives twice**: over MQTT (`main/mqtt-client.js:8`) and through the backend WebSocket (`routes/alarms.js:126`).
- **Three different de-duplication heuristics**: `alarm-history.js:76-157` merges on "any shared node, no time limit".
- **Missing alarm lifecycle:** no hysteresis, no persistence filter, no return-to-normal, and no ADVISORY level.
- **ACK is not persisted, and one ACK hides the banner for every alarm** (`alarm-banner.js:14-16`).
- **SILENCE is a no-op:** there is no `ipcMain` handler, and the `preload.js:11` arity is wrong.
- **No audio.** The dispatch log is pre-filled with fake rows (`dispatch-log.js:9-14`).
- **Escalation overwrites the record.** A WARNING→CRITICAL escalation overwrites the same `ALM-Nxx` row, so there is no escalation history.

**6. Thresholds exist in 4–6 copies that disagree.**
- Strain: 5.3/6.6 mm/m in `constants.py`, 4000/5300 µε in `sensors.py:564`, and 500/1500 µε in `node-sensors.js:321` (**10× lower**). The node-detail panel therefore shows "CRITICAL" while the marker stays green.
- Tilt: 3/10 mm/m in the engine, 400/1200 mdeg in `node-sensors.js:157`.
- Pore pressure, settlement and fissure have no thresholds at all.

**7. Cracks have no classes.**
- `width_mm` is really strain in mm/m (`forge/states.py:187-221`), and it is never shown.
- The FORGE crack sliders, the map crack colours and the "CRACKMETER FISSURE" readout have no legend or explanation.
- The finale already has a physically based crack model to reuse (`minesim/cracks.py:79-100`):
  - opening = (e1 − 3000 µε) × 8 m spacing, on the principal tensile strain
  - cracks latch and never heal
- The dashboard has no ⓘ component. The React side has `InfoBadge`/`InfoModal` (`InfoModal.tsx:11-56`).

**8. Maths defects that change numbers:**
- **Collapse strain:** `collapse.py:196-199` applies the panel's B (69 m) to a 35 m pit. The result is **−338 mm/m** strain, which int16 clips to 32.7.
- **One cave-in flags most of the district:** a single 100 m / 10 m FORGE cave-in flags 21 of 31 nodes, 6 CRITICAL and 15 WARNING (measured through `/forge/frame`). The Gaussian tail still gives > 3 mm/m of slope at 2.5R.
- **Sensor noise:** `sensors.py:427-430`. The noise is 7× its budget: the OU wander std is about 9.5 µε against 1.33 budgeted.
- **Vibration:** `sensors.py:487-490` adds PPV to RMS, and no node attenuates with distance.
- **Strain direction:** `sensors.py:418,497` ignore `strain_y`.
- **Pore pressure and moisture:** `sensors.py:512,530` are invented linear functions of S.
- **Temperature:** diurnal only, and it never drives tilt drift.
- **SNR boot gate:** `session.py:270-289` / `gates.py` use the wrong units and always pass.
- **Crack latch reset:** `session.py:443-448` clears the sensor crack latch every tick.
- **Disk:** `simulation/out/simulation_packets/` is **17 GB** of JSON (`save_packet_json` defaults to True). It is gitignored.

**9. The "blue layout" on the SIM tab is empty navy space.**
- The SIM tab (`index.html:610-708`) has only the 3D viewport and a 340 px right column that is stripes until a node is clicked.
- The navy is `MineViewport.tsx:371`, the camera starts at `[430,400,500]` (`:360`), and the HUD overlaps the iframe header.

## Recommendations beyond this plan (for Adarsh to pick later)
1. **Forecast versus measured is the professional core.** Above an active longwall, a metre of subsidence is *expected*. Add a residual alarm: measured minus the fitted forecast, beyond 3σ. It catches what the model did not predict, where absolute limits only report the obvious.
2. **Put three labels on every view:** LIVE (measured) / FORECAST / SCENARIO (FORGE). Show the forecast horizon and an uncertainty band. The finale's GP gives σ: RMS 37.7 mm against 52.1 mm for physics alone.
3. **Role views from the PS (S14):**
   - Operator: alarms, what to do
   - Planner: forecasts, face plan
   - Regulator: audit trail, export
4. **SMS and email with retry and per-role routing (S13).** Real mobile or web alert reach (S16).
5. **Filter alarms against the blast log** (DGMS 7/1997): suppress vibration-correlated tilt spikes within ±2 min of a logged blast, and say so on the alarm.
6. **Get real data (R4 of RECOMMENDATIONS.md):**
   - LiCSBAS/COMET InSAR over Godavari Valley
   - NSW end-of-panel survey reports for real tilt and strain
   - 24–48 h of logs from one hardware node, to replace the noise guesses
7. **Face advance from a mine log** (stoppages, weekends) instead of a constant 4 m/d. This needs a numerical time model, not the closed form.
8. **Heartbeat timers disagree** (30 s in `node-markers.js:7`, 60 s in `live-provider.js:14`); unify them in the thresholds file.
9. **Resolve the 52 `OPEN — VERIFY` parameters** in finale `assumptions.yaml`, starting with the 5.3 mm/m "DGMS" strain limit, which has no cited circular.
