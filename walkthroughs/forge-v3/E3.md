# E3 — FORGE 3D look: zone rings, event colour ramp, cracks on the deformed ground

## What changed
- **Zones.** `forge-frame` passes `frame.zones` to the new `EventZones` component. Per zone it draws a white ring at `r_white` and a red ring at `r_red`, a 20% red fill inside the white ring and a 20% yellow fill in the band. A crack zone is the same shapes as capsules around the polyline. Every vertex is re-sampled from `sampleGroundY` (the height `TerrainMesh` writes, exaggeration included), so the rings follow a pit down.
- **Preview.** `forge-preview` draws the same shapes dashed, at 1.2 R and 1.5 R (10% fills). For a crack the `line` now carries `width_m`. `TargetBeacon` keeps its beam and reticle in the FORGE slot but no longer draws its own rings (`showRings={false}`).
- **Colour.** `TerrainMesh` colours two drops separately:
  - bowl (`state.dropDistanceM`): the blue `DEPTH_STOPS` over `BOWL_RAMP_MAX_M` = 2.25 m (a·m), contours every 0.25 m;
  - event (`serverPerturbDrop`, from 2 cm up): new `HOT_STOPS` (#FFE066 → #FF9A1F → #E8341C → #8B0A0A → #3D0000) at `t = ln(1 + d/0.05) / ln(1 + 25/0.05)`, contours every 1 m.
  `TerrainLegend` shows both ramps, the event one with log-spaced ticks.
- **Cracks.** `frame.cracks` → `setCrackLines`. `CrackLinesOverlay` (and the dashed preview line) sample the deformed surface at each end, +0.5 m.
- **Looks** (`playForgeCaveInEffect`, new `playForgeCrackEffect`):
  - cave-in: dust and shake, as before;
  - tilt: no dust, a small shake (0.25), and a terrain arrow at the target along `direction_deg`, held 4 s, then a 1 s fade;
  - crack: a thin dust line along the segment, no shake (the CRACK fire now sends a `forge-effect` of type `crack` with its `line`);
  - vibration: unchanged.

## Notes
- `S_MAX_FULL_M` in `geomechanicsEngine.ts` is 5 m, not 2.25 m. The bowl ramp uses its own constant `BOWL_RAMP_MAX_M = A_SUBS * M_SEAM_M`, and the engine ceilings are untouched.
- The bowl term is `state.dropDistanceM`, which also holds local collapses in the standalone app and the SIM slot. A collapse deeper than 2.25 m there now saturates the blue ramp (it used to be normalised to 50 m).
- `verify_bowl_physics.mjs` had a hand-copied composite that still normalised to 50 m; it now uses `BOWL_RAMP_MAX_M` and gains checks for the hot ramp.
- Tilt has no zone in the frame (`hazard_zones` covers cave-ins and cracks only), so a tilt shows the arrow and the hot bowl, no rings.
- The `verify_forge_*` scripts overwrite `walkthroughs/forge-v2/img/*.png`; restore them with `git checkout` unless the change is intended.
