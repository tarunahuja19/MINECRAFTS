# P3 — stream the moving bowl profile to the 3D views

## What changed
- Before this step the 3D view never received `S` at all: it drew the baseline bowl as `base_bowl_mesh × timeScalar` (`base_bowl_mesh` = `surface._BASE_S[::2, ::2]` sent once by `/config`, `timeScalar` sent every tick). With P2's travelling face, the bowl's *shape* changes over time, not just its size, so that formula stopped matching the physics — and it turns out `base_bowl_mesh`/`baseBowlMesh` was being fetched into state but never actually read by the renderer, so the 3D view drew no baseline bowl at all before this step.
- `surface.py` adds `bowl_px_wire()` (the static x profile, 121 floats) and `bowl_py_wire(t)` (the time-varying y profile, 121 floats, rounded to 1e-4).
- `sandbox/server.py`: `GET /config` and the WebSocket init payload both add `"bowl_px"` alongside the existing `base_bowl_mesh`.
- `sandbox/session.py`: the live tick payload adds `"bowl_py"` and `"face_y_m"` (`surface.face_y(t_sim_days)`, rounded to 0.1 m).
- `forge/server.py`: the FORGE frame adds the same two fields.
- Frontend: `geomechanicsEngine.ts`'s `LiveGeomechanicsEngine` gains `setBowlPx`/`setBowlPy` and a linear `sampleProfile`; every ground sampler (terrain mesh, node markers, beacon, inspector) now reads `drop += sampleProfile(bowlPx, x) * sampleProfile(bowlPy, y)` from one shared engine instance instead of each view re-deriving its own baseline. `App.tsx` holds `bowlPx`/`bowlPy`/`faceYM` in state, sets `bowlPx` once from `/config`/WS-init and updates `bowlPy`/`faceYM` on every tick and FORGE frame (`applyBowl`), and passes `bowlPy` down to the viewport. Without a `bowl_py` (an older server) the ground draws with no baseline bowl, as before.
- SIM HUD: `sim-embed.js` dispatches a `sim-face` DOM event with `faceYM` on every tick for the `sim` slot; `sim-tab.js` listens and writes `#sim-hud-face` as `"<round> m N"`.
- Screenshots of FORGE on day 40 and day 200 (`walkthroughs/forge-v3/img/forge-bowl-day-{40,120,200}.png`) show the bowl visibly further north by day 200.
