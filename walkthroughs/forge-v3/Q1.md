# Q1 — no default target ring in the embedded views

## What changed
- `App.tsx` initialised `targetLocation` to `(120, 80)` and `beaconHidden` to `false` regardless of embed mode, so both the SIM tab and FORGE tab iframes (`?embed=1&slot=sim|forge`) drew a red 75 m `TargetBeacon` ring on first paint that nobody had asked for.
- `beaconHidden` now starts `true` whenever `isEmbed`.
- SIM slot (`embedParams.slot === "sim"`): the beacon becomes visible only on a `trigger` embed command (a live collapse fired from the parent). A terrain click in the SIM iframe (`handleTerrainClick`) no longer reveals it — that path still posts the `terrain-target` event to the parent, it just skips `setBeaconHidden(false)`.
- FORGE slot: unchanged — visible after a terrain click or a `forge-preview` command, hidden again as before.
- Standalone (non-embed) app: unchanged, beacon still starts visible.
