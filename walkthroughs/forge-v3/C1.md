# C1 — scenario script format and validated loader

## What changed
- `simulation/scenarios/default_demo.json`: `{name, seed, base_iso_time, duration_days: 365, tick_seconds, events}`. Events use the E1 shapes (`sandbox/events.py`), each with a `day`:

  | day | event | where | reaches (FORGE rule, day 365) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 60 | crack (-110,-70) → (60,10), throw 0.4 m, width 10 m, opens over 3 d | across the 1C fault transect | N22, N23 CRITICAL, N05 WARNING |
  | 120 | tilt at (145,0), 5 mm/m toward 90°, radius 60 m, over 10 d | east side | N06, N17, N24 WARNING |
  | 150 | vibration 45 mm/s, 60 s | site-wide | none (moves no state) |
  | 200 | cave-in at (-200,120), R 35 m, depth 6 m | north-west, on the 1B rib | N11 CRITICAL, N01, N12 WARNING |

  Total: 6 WARNING and 3 CRITICAL.
- `simulation/sandbox/script.py`: `load_script(name | path)`, `parse_script(dict)`, `available_scripts()`, `ScriptError`.
  - Ranges are the FORGE sliders' (cave-in depth 0.5–25 m, radius 10–300 m; tilt rate 0.5–30, radius 10–500, over 1–30 d; crack throw 0.05–2, width 2–30, open 0.5–30 d; vibration 1–100 mm/s).
  - Positions must be inside ±300 m, `day` inside `[0, duration_days]`. Unknown fields, missing fields, booleans and NaN are rejected.
  - Events come back sorted by day (stable) with defaults filled in (`duration_h` 4.8, `warning_hours` 8, vibration `duration_s` 60), so they go straight into `to_failures`.
  - Every error is `events[<index>].<field>: <what is wrong>`. The name must match `[a-z0-9_-]+`, so a name can't walk out of `scenarios/`.
- `tick_seconds` is new (not in the plan). See C2 for why.
