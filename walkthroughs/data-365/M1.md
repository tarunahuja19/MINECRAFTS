# M1 — event shape fix: local B_event instead of panel-scale B_HORIZ

## What changed
This step started with the code fix already sitting uncommitted in the
working tree from a paused run (`sandbox/collapse.py`), matching the plan's
M1 bullet exactly. It had no walkthrough, no named constants, and — as the
plan itself warned — none of the forge-v3 tests whose expected numbers
change had been updated, so `pytest` was 9 failures short of green. This
step promoted the two literals to named, sourced constants and brought
every dependent test (and the frontend's TENSILE WAVE geometry solver,
which inverts the same kernel) back in line with the corrected physics.

- **`sandbox/collapse.py`**: a localized event's Gaussian footprint now
  uses `sigma = EVENT_SIGMA_FRAC * radius_m` (0.5, was `radius_m` — sigma
  equal to R left the tail still > 3 mm/m of tilt at 2.5R, flagging nodes
  far outside the drawn rings). Its strain now uses its own local
  coefficient `b_event = B_EVENT_COEFF * radius_m` (0.4) instead of the
  panel-scale `B_HORIZ` (69 m, derived from the 375 m seam depth) — using
  the regional depth-of-influence coefficient on a small pit's sharp
  curvature is what produced strain in the hundreds of mm/m and clipped
  the int16 `strain_ue` channel.
- **`sandbox/constants.py`**: `EVENT_SIGMA_FRAC = 0.5` and
  `B_EVENT_COEFF = 0.4` (both ASSUMED), replacing the bare literals so the
  frontend and every test derive from the same two numbers.
- **Both relations the frontend's `solveStrainIntervention` inverts moved**,
  because b_event now carries its own factor of R where the old B_HORIZ was
  R-independent:
  - the tensile peak now lands at `sqrt(3)/2 * R` (was `sqrt(3) * R`) —
    sigma halved, and the second derivative's extremum is always at
    `sqrt(3) * sigma`;
  - peak strain now falls off as `magnitude * C / R` (was `C / R^2`) — the
    R in `b_event = B_EVENT_COEFF * R` cancels one power of the `1/R^2` a
    Gaussian's curvature peak gives. `C = B_EVENT_COEFF * 8 * e^(-3/2) *
    1000 ≈ 714.02`, derived the same way the old `2 * e^(-3/2)` was, with
    the `8` coming from substituting `sigma = R/2` into the curvature
    peak's `2/sigma^2`.
  `frontend/src/utils/geomechanicsEngine.ts` gets its own
  `B_EVENT_COEFF = 0.4`, `TENSILE_PEAK_RADIUS_RATIO = sqrt(3)/2`, and
  `STRAIN_GEOMETRY_C` derived from it; `solveStrainIntervention` inverts
  the new `/R` relation instead of `/R^2`.
- **Nine tests updated** to the corrected numbers (all re-derived and
  cross-checked against the running kernel, not hand-typed):
  - `test_collapse_consistency.py::test_settled_peak_slope` — a 0.5 m/100 m
    pit's peak tilt is now 6.07 mm/m (sigma = 50 m, was 3.03 mm/m at
    sigma = 100 m).
  - `test_session_d_e.py::test_gate_t53_intervention_propagation` — an 0.8 m
    magnitude/60 m radius event's settled `delta_s` at 60 m is now the
    2-sigma point (0.108 m, sigma = 30 m), and at 120 m the 4-sigma point
    (~0.0003 m, effectively flat) — both were 1-sigma/2-sigma before.
  - `test_script.py` (renamed
    `test_demo_reaches_warnings_and_a_critical`) — the default demo's
    honest end-of-run count is 2 WARNING / 3 CRITICAL (measured), not the
    inflated 3+/1+ the panel-scale strain produced by leaking far outside
    the drawn rings.
  - `test_scripted_run.py::test_tilt_alarms_come_from_the_tilt_not_a_ring` —
    the tilt event alone can now reach CRITICAL (peak tilt roughly doubled
    from the sigma halving), not just WARNING, so the assertion accepts
    both.
  - `test_render_placement.py` (5 tests) and `test_strain_calibration.py`
    (the TypeScript cross-language guard, 4 constant/relation tests) — the
    peak-location ratio, the `C` constant and its `/R` vs `/R^2` law, and
    the two end-to-end TENSILE WAVE intervention scenarios (`_settled_payload`,
    the breach case, and the capped case) all re-derived and re-verified
    against the live kernel. The capped-ring ring-tolerance grew from 45 m
    to 70 m because M1's steeper `1/R` falloff pushed the "capped and
    genuinely under the DGMS limit" ring out to ~270 m, 65 m from the only
    axis-aligned pair of strain-carrying monuments (~205 m) — there is no
    other strain-carrying node closer to the axis to aim at instead.
- **The plan's own two checks, added as a new permanent test**
  (`test_collapse_consistency.py`):
  - the session's default `apply_collapse` (radius 60 m, magnitude 0.75 m)
    now peaks at 28.28 mm/m — under the plan's 30 mm/m ceiling (was in the
    hundreds).
  - a 100 m radius / 10 m deep FORGE cave-in now flags **8 of 31 nodes**
    non-normal (5 CRITICAL, 3 WARNING) — down from 21 (6 CRITICAL, 15
    WARNING) before the fix, comfortably under the plan's ≤12 target.
