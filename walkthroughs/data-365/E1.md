# E1 — seeded, deterministic 365-day mine environment

## What changed
This step started with the module and its test suite already sitting
complete and untracked in the working tree from a paused run
(`sandbox/environment.py`, `tests/test_environment.py`), matching the
plan's E1 bullet in full. It had no walkthrough and was not part of any
commit. Each section was read against the plan's spec line by line and
cross-checked with the full test suite before committing as-is — no
rewrite was needed.

- **`sandbox/environment.py`** (new), `build_year(seed, ...) ->
  EnvironmentYear`: a pure, seeded (`np.random.default_rng(seed)`),
  8760-hour (365-day, day 0 = 2026-01-01 UTC) environmental context for
  the 31-node Adriyala network:
  - **Air temperature** — Ramagundam/Peddapalli monthly normals (IMD,
    VERIFY) plus an 08:30 UTC diurnal cycle (±6 °C, VERIFY) and an AR(1)
    weather anomaly (φ=0.92, σ=1 °C, ASSUMED).
  - **Rainfall** — monthly normals centred on ~850 mm/yr with June–
    September carrying the southwest monsoon (IMD, VERIFY), stochastic
    wet/dry days per month.
  - **Pore pressure** (Tier 2B boreholes only, NaN elsewhere) — a 294 kPa
    hydrostatic base (30 m head) plus a linear-reservoir response to rain
    (τ=10 d) and 0.5 kPa observation noise.
  - **Moisture** (Tier 1C fault-transect only, NaN elsewhere) — a 10%
    dry-season base, τ=5 d drainage, capped at 45%.
  - **Blasts** — one development blast per working day at 07:30 UTC (none
    Sundays or the 10 gazetted 2026 mine holidays), 20–60 kg, positioned
    relative to the active face.
  - **PPV / dominant frequency** — USBM RI 8507 scaled-distance
    attenuation (K=1140, β=1.6, VERIFY), frequency falling from 40 Hz at
    375 m depth toward 10 Hz with range; `EnvironmentYear.ppv()` is an
    instance-method forwarder to the module function.
  - **Device faults** — battery drains 3.60→3.30 V over 120–200 days then
    is logged as replaced; 1–3% per-node mesh packet loss; exactly one
    node's 5-day outage in August; one 6-hour gateway (Tier 3) outage; one
    Tier 1B strain gauge stuck for 3 days; all as a chronological
    `events` log alongside the raw arrays.
  - Every constant carries its basis inline (`ASSUMED` or a cited source
    marked `VERIFY`), per the plan's rule that no new constant is
    unsourced.
- **`tests/test_environment.py`** (new), 15 tests: seed reproducibility
  (identical output same seed, different output different seed), rain
  total in 600–1100 mm with ≥70% in JJAS, May mean ≥ Jan mean + 8 °C,
  pore-pressure/moisture tier isolation (NaN off-tier, in-range on-tier,
  August–April pore-pressure delta ≥10 kPa), blast count 300±20 with
  correct day-of-week/holiday/hour/charge/depth constraints, PPV
  finite/monotonically falling with a 10–40 Hz frequency band, the three
  device-fault scenarios each occurring exactly once with the right
  duration, packet loss in [1%, 3%] per node and overall, and `build_year`
  completing in under 3 seconds.

No plan deviations and no bugs found — this step is a verification pass,
walkthrough and commit, not new implementation.
